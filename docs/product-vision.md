# OpenOPC Product Vision

OpenOPC enables one owner to organize multiple governed AI roles, produce
verifiable outcomes, and compound reviewed execution into organizational
memory.

## Initial product focus

The initial outcome-validation scope is:

1. software delivery;
2. content production;
3. evidence-backed research.

These are the workloads in the versioned Task-versus-Company benchmark.
Additional organization templates are extension surfaces, not production
claims.

## Product contract

- **Task Mode** is the direct path for bounded work owned by one execution
  agent.
- **Company Mode** is the coordinated path for work that benefits from
  decomposition, parallel role ownership, independent review, and final
  integration.
- **Mission Control** is the operator surface for durable runs, approvals,
  provider readiness, budgets, learning evidence, and recovery.
- **Self-Grown** changes future runs only after reviewed evidence. Learning
  never expands permissions or rewrites an active run.

## North-star outcomes

OpenOPC optimizes for:

- accepted outcome rate and quality;
- evidence completeness;
- bounded cost and duration;
- fewer interventions and rework cycles;
- successful recovery without duplicate side effects;
- measured lift from promoted learning assets.

Agent count, message volume, and autonomous activity are diagnostics, not
success metrics.

## Non-goals

OpenOPC does not claim:

- that Company Mode is always preferable to Task Mode;
- that an available template proves readiness for an industry;
- that unknown subscription usage is free;
- that simulation or model self-grading is production evidence;
- that learning may bypass human, safety, permission, or release gates.

The product claim becomes stronger only when the checked-in benchmark,
independent judgments, provider readiness campaign, and promotion dossier pass.
